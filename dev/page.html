The Release Train

How we ship: a field guide to release trains

12 minute read

Every team eventually builds a ritual around shipping. Ours started as a checklist taped to a monitor and grew into a weekly train with a conductor, a manifest and a hard departure time.

The train leaves on Tuesdays. Anything merged by Monday evening is on it. Anything else waits a week, and that wait is the whole point: it turns a negotiation into a schedule.

We keep the manifest boring. Each entry names the change, the owner and the rollback. If the rollback is "revert the commit", we write that down too, because the 2am version of you will want it spelled out.

The conductor rotates. They own the departure, not the cargo. When something is not ready they do not argue about it, they move it to next week's manifest and the train still leaves on time.
